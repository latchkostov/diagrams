## App name

Live Diagram for Confluence

## Tagline

Bring system diagrams to life on your Confluence pages. Press Play to see messages flow.

## Summary

View and play distributed-system diagrams in Confluence Cloud. Choose a page attachment, then press Play to follow the message flow or a saved simulation. Free, with no analytics or external data transfers.

## More details

Explain how your system works, right beside the words that describe it.

Export a .diagram.json file from Live Diagrams, insert /Live Diagram on a published Confluence page, and choose or upload the attachment. The diagram stays still until a reader presses Play. Replay Free play or a saved simulation, and maximise the view when you need more room.

To make changes, download the attachment, edit it in Live Diagrams, and replace the selected attachment. Confluence keeps its version history. The macro shows the latest version when loaded.

Readers use their own page and attachment permissions. Diagram files stay in Confluence; viewing and playback run in the browser with Forge-hosted resources. No analytics, external requests or outbound links in the macro.

Free and open source. View and play only; editing happens outside Confluence. Documentation and support links below explain setup and troubleshooting.
