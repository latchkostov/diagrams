## Held connection Inspector can reclaim focus after Escape

Lane C stops on this product race. Keep the immediate typing/Escape regression and its exact label and focus assertions; waiting for the field before typing would stop exercising the promised pending-rename behavior.

The fresh [60-run CI report](../design-review/2026-10-25-flake-tail-ci-evidence.md) identifies two failing attempts of `held connection lazyEdgePanel chunk preserves rename keys and Escape`: [40160789032](https://github.com/latchkostov/diagrams/actions/runs/40160789032/job/121071925132) and [40174957924](https://github.com/latchkostov/diagrams/actions/runs/40174957924/job/121113693337). Both retain the exact typed name but leave `inspector-field-label` focused after Escape and chunk release.

Reproduction uses the lockfile-pinned `mcr.microsoft.com/playwright:v1.64.1-noble` image, a production E2E build, six workers sharing three CPUs, zero retries and `--repeat-each 30`. The neighbouring held `EdgePanel` Escape case reproduces the same focus failure, while typing, Enter and node naming provide controls.

On baseline `972aab1f8dff3cfabeb18fc52b7dc0eae43369e3`, `lazyEdgePanel` Escape fails **4/30**, `EdgePanel` Escape fails **30/30**, and each of the other seven inspector variants passes **30/30**. Reports: `tests/.output/g/rTppzvM/e1/results.json`. A temporary diagnostic reproduces the neighbour **2/2** and attaches the interaction journal (`tests/.output/g/rnK9JvZ/e1/results.json`); the diagnostic is removed from the final tree.

The journal before chunk release contains `inspector.field` and `rename.hold`, but **no `rename.typed:escape`** after the Escape press. After release it records `rename.handed` with command `type` (without `:escape`), then focuses the field and its frame retry; there is no `naming.done`. The browser trace contains the focused edge's `edge-messages-call` tooltip before release. The likely cause is Escape priority: `useInteractionLifecycle` calls `controller.escape()` and `stopImmediatePropagation()` before the later pending-rename capture listener; `MessageTypesCard` registers its dismiss handler through `attachEscape`. Thus the carried-message tooltip can consume Escape intended to finish pending naming. Loading that tooltip independently explains the shell-held variant's intermittent result. Confirm that ownership ordering in the product fix, including already mounted fields and viewer-only tooltip dismissal.

Follow up in the product lane: trace pending type-ahead completion, `naming.done` and subsequent focus moves while the deferred connection Inspector mounts. Preserve the UX contract that Escape saves the typed name and returns focus to the named canvas item. Do not add a test wait that removes the held-field interleaving.
