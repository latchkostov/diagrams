## Engine ledger review fixes (R1)

Restore master throughput when sampled pool calls reply during a caller pause or
have nested sampled delays. Reservation admission clocks remain immutable; the
ledger calculates residence from elapsed time and child delay in the same clock
domain, then translates the remaining hold into the owner's active clock.
Positive fractional connections are admitted with a float-residue epsilon, and
releasing the last fraction clears numerical pool residue.

Eight differential cases record every delivery and completion at each 10 ms
simulation tick against master `354b5dd0b12771d1fc4c16a51285ea810cbf55a4`.
They cover nested calls, pause/resume, deadlines during a pause, and scales 1.01,
1.1 and 1.5. The trace fixture was generated by temporarily loading that commit's
`src/sim/engine.ts` alongside the current engine and running the same
`ledgerTraceCases` and `ledgerTrace` helper; the temporary master engine was removed.
The command generator now includes fractional scales, with its reduced cleanup
counterexample retained as a regression. Heap cancellation tests count element
reads and reject a linear search, including removals that repair the heap.

R1 preserves master's wall-time pool reply deadlines. An unconditional pause
freeze was rejected because master can expire an existing hold during a long
pause and dispatch another call; changing that policy would violate R1's behavior
preservation requirement. The previous ledger-only test expecting a long pause
to freeze that deadline was corrected, and differential cases pin both sides of
the deadline. No output goldens are changed or re-recorded.
