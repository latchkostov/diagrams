## Faster panning on large diagrams

Held mouse pans capture their pointer, avoiding repeated browser hit tests across every card and SVG path.
Live badges subscribe to visible membership and zoom rather than each translation; unchanged membership also
skips accessibility DOM scans. Off-screen readouts still receive each publication, and entering cards fit their
current identity in the first animation frame, before paint, outside the input handler.
Camera-owned fit completion still includes identity geometry, and pending pan fitting survives incoming measurements.

At the benchmark's 2× CPU throttle, fitted Edit pan improves 41→26 ms/step at 500 nodes and 87→66 at 1,500;
frame p90 improves 33→17 and 67→50 ms. Running Live at 1,500 improves 694→481 ms/step. The 30 ms target
at 1,500 is still open; node dragging is essentially unchanged. Detailed samples, traces and rejected prototypes
are in [the design review](../design-review/2026-10-28-pan-scale.md).

No golden is updated; the output parity contract is unchanged.
