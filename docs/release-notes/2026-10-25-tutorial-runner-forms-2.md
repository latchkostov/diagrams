## Tutorials: runner forms for builds 06 to 12

The tutorial format gains the remaining forms the Simulation basics, Waiting & threads, Resilience and Design studies
lessons need, all as typed data:

- **Live's own controls as demos:** slow motion (from a node, or the next message), Inject ▸ 1/10/100, the bottleneck's
  Apply fix, Live's inputs (Traffic ×, Capacity ×, Scale), opening Inputs, the event log, Simulations… or the capacity
  overlay, and continuing a paused narration note once the run reaches it.
- **Saved simulations:** a demo authors one as Simulations… would (Drill steps from fault presets, expectations),
  keeping what a learner already saved under that id. Checks find one by what it has, saved or running.
- **Checks:** slow motion finished; a Fault cleared by Restore; an expectation either way; Live's panels and hover card
  as Live publishes them; Live's inputs; nested readings (latency percentiles and wait/work time, threads, a fleet's
  starting replicas); numbers the diagram says (partitions against replicas); sums over the nodes inside a boundary.
- **Design studies:** `study: true` lessons point only after Show me. Show me never moves the camera while slow motion
  plays.

`sinceStep` baseline tells a remounted Live session from the run it was read in; Continue replays a `sinceStep` step
that has a demo; a retried control that notifies synchronously is judged once it returns. A Lab lesson plays a
