## Hover and Live cards

Hovering a card that cannot be placed no longer takes Enter from a focused Live control. Focused summaries stay mounted while the pointer moves, and Tab crosses readouts in read-only embeds and Interactive HTML exports. Removing a node or connection closes its pinned card; Undo restores the target without restoring the pin. Items without Live details never own a hidden readout.

Pinned cards rank their complete section list before showing problem details. Inspector defaults select an available section, and a card follows its moving node or connection without requiring a pan. Compact summaries retain three rows; clipping coverage checks the rows inside their actual container. Imported edge identifiers with malformed UTF-16 retain their original values and dispatch without URI encoding.
