## Line editing and route reliability

Cancelling image export now stops its pending route-readiness polling. Cancelled connections release their gesture on matching touch or primary pointer cancellation, and a late dot-resolver download cannot revive an old connection.

Automatic line nudges check every relevant saved segment before taking a slot, using indexed track/span queries even for long saved routes. Reversing a connection preserves the geometric interpretation of its bends, including diagonal and symmetric imported input. Interior bends stay absolute; end joins retain their existing port-relative behavior.

Browser regressions also cover configured vendor route tables through an inspector stencil change and one-step Undo. No controls or text were added. The two existing live-preview/segment-identity quarantines remain open.

Golden inventory: none changed. `pnpm parity` checked 3,151 goldens with zero changed, added, missing or stale records; all 1,858 Node and 108 Linux browser cases passed. No golden was re-recorded.
