## Smaller canvas and Live downloads

The canvas first view drops from 374300 B to 370641 B gzip, leaving 4167 B below the unchanged reserve ceiling. Live's additional download drops from 131614 B to 128512 B, leaving 6488 B of headroom. The preferred 8192 B Live floor would require another 1704 B; first-frame rendering and playback remain synchronous while the separate engine split is pending.

The closed stencil library downloads its contents on opening. Its loading search accepts text and preserves focus through arrival or Retry; modal focus returns before its input is replaced, and Escape clears search before closing the flyout. Gallery handoff and exports keep working while it downloads. Capacity-panel and recording-format helpers load with their existing optional surfaces, and phone overflow controls load with their menu.

All 3178 parity goldens are unchanged; none is re-recorded. Budgets, reserves and the skill ZIP cap are unchanged. Full before/after measurements for every JavaScript chunk are in [the design review](../design-review/2026-10-27-trim-first-view-session.md).
