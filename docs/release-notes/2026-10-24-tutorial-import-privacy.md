## Tutorial imports and recovery

Tutorials now use the app's normal import and open actions. Imported diagrams retain their privacy flags,
the private-by-default preference, source provenance and save destination. Share-link imports are saved
in the Shared slot before opening, and storage failures prevent navigation. Opening a document leaves the
tutorial without reloading, preserving device file handles; New diagram explicitly opens a fresh draft.

Your diagrams keeps deletion Undo inside its dialog, accessible from the keyboard. Fault demonstrations
wait for the Live session and its enabled action, and Restart ends slow motion before replaying the
tutorial's simulation. Example-download recovery tests intercept both development and build modules;
numeric checks cover values below, at and above their thresholds.

No output goldens change.
