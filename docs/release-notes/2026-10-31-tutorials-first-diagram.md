## First diagram and keeping work

Three new lessons teach the work around a diagram: **Find your way around** (`workbench-tour`),
**Keep and reuse a diagram** (`library-reuse`) and **Review what an import inferred**
(`import-and-review`). They sit before drawing and after the command palette respectively. The import
lesson supplies an invented public Compose fixture, runs the real parser on this device and asks the
learner to review its report, correct an ambiguous worker and acknowledge the provenance/privacy limits.

The mobile/API, quick-add, dots-and-sides, Properties, annotations and command-palette lessons have
revised instructions and version 2 progress. Connecting the first diagram is a real independent action;
quick-add ends with an unassisted search; reconnecting checks the same edge's label, relationship and
source-side setting. Properties ends on the documented card, annotations distinguish zones from
membership, and the palette includes an actual Properties action. The five retained drawing exercises
keep their existing exercises and versions; boundaries and arrangement retain their explicit product
keyboard gaps.

Independent Skip additions use unconnected editor creation, so an earlier selection cannot silently
complete the connection exercise. The unassisted search checks PostgreSQL rather than any store.
Workbench pointer practice deliberately pans out of Following; its keyboard route uses the camera control.

Practice edits remain temporary. Only the learner's **Save a copy** action in `library-reuse` creates
an owned browser entry, and graduation requires seeing that entry in Your diagrams. File Save/Save as
downloads a diagram file without acquiring ownership. The import lesson replaces its sandbox instead
of opening an owned diagram. Failed or cancelled work cannot complete a step, and Restart, Exit and
replacement cancel pending work. Ordinary Open outside that opt-in destination keeps its normal
handoff, file handles and provenance.

Long instructions can be scrolled from the keyboard on a phone or short screen. Compact Restart/Exit
controls keep their translated accessible names. Pointer and keyboard paths, Skip and versioned
Continue use document checks and typed product observations, not test-only actions.
