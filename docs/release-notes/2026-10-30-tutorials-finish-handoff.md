## Lesson finish: logged, and what’s next

Finishing a tutorial opens a quiet, non-modal sheet below the step strip. It shows the saved completion date, chapter progress, newly earned seals and the next unfinished lesson’s title, summary, minutes and full step list. Start opens that lesson directly, following chapter order and returning to earlier unfinished lessons when needed. Finishing every lesson shows the final syllabus seal.

Save a copy stays in the strip. Escape or Stay on this diagram dismisses the sheet and focuses the practice canvas. Exit, Pick another chapter and All tutorials return to the hub with the current lesson’s chapter open and its row in view, preserving history-based navigation. The sheet focuses its heading, puts Start one Tab away, and keeps the canvas available behind it. Phone layouts keep the heading and actions visible while the next lesson’s steps scroll.

Parity: only `tests/parity/goldens/node/i18n/catalogs.json` changes. Twelve new `tutorials.handoff.*` messages increase message counts by twelve and English fallback counts by twelve in non-English catalogs; tutorial namespace fingerprints change accordingly. Existing lesson text, diagram outputs and image goldens are unchanged.
