## Tutorial UX review

The designer-agent review found no new chrome or navigation concerns. Three goals asked readers
to inspect counters outside the highlighted hover card. The final goals now refer to the visible
Answer model readout, and queue recovery highlights the new consumer for its Processed count.
The strict checks still verify both shared endpoints, independent payments, and the bounded queue.

Rejected keeping a slow reader in the backlog fixture: Trouble correctly offers Inputs there,
not Add a consumer. Rejected cross-run backlog deltas: adding a consumer rebuilds Live and resets
its counters. The lesson states that restart and proves new processing before completion.
The two rate-limit examples have independent sandboxes and catalog/metadata registrations.
