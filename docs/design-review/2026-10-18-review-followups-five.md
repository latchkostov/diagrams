A paused forward seek must show the destination, so an idle destination edge loses a held highlight immediately while an edge carrying a destination message stays active. Continuous playback retains its existing three-publish hold. Both paused and playing controller-to-decorator tests cover that rule.

The stencil drag picture stays fixed and offscreen, leaving the palette search, result cards and canvas geometry unchanged during dragstart. The production-browser regression rejects reversed stylesheet imports. Camera movement, focus, selection and menus remain owned by the other branches.

Benchmark changes affect measurement only. Capture uses the same lifecycle for the hook and panel paths and samples while the panel close is pending. An unstable timeout is reported honestly instead of appearing to settle immediately. Machine-speed budgets and local heavy benchmarks were rejected in favour of fake frame traces and deterministic gate assertions.
