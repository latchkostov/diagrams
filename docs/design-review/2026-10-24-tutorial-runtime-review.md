## Tutorial runtime review

The existing tutorial strip keeps the same controls and height. Show me remains available while its hint is expanded, keeps keyboard focus, identifies its associated hint and announces the requested help. Canvas reveals continue through the existing controller's focus policy.

A completed naming step stays settled while its expected name holds. Changing that subject's name makes it the first unfinished step again. Skip is scoped to the displayed step and its actions are replaced on step changes; a double-click cannot activate the following step. Fixed delays and debouncing were rejected because transition guards express the rule without timing assumptions. No new chrome or output golden changes are needed.
