blocking caller's displayed throughput. Queue time already paced by a narrower processor
does not stretch again at its caller. No controls, wording, placement or chrome change.

The regression checks every node outcome and call count with the existing I1 allowance,
including the unrelated source, and retains the scale-one reference.
