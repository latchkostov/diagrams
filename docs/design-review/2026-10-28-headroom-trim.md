## Headroom trim UX review

The quality-loop designer review found no change to visible chrome, cursor, focus, keyboard,
loading/retry behavior or export policy. Native private member conversions preserve playback and
rendering bodies. The five viewer exclusions remove only unreachable editing controls: read-only
canvases disable growing and connecting, and the planning authoring panels require an editor.

No new lazy surface is introduced, so there is no new pending, failed-download or focus boundary to
exercise with a held chunk. Existing slow-motion, inspector, narration, drill and export integration
specs cover the retained behavior. Pixel probes and diagnostic attributes remain in development and
E2E builds; production playback does not consume them.
