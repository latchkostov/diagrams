## Edit together E5: presence

- Others are told apart by name as well as colour: a name tag on a person's first outlined item and beside their
  pointer. Outlines sit 4 px outside the item, so your own selection ring stays visible inside a peer's.
- Nothing a peer does moves your view, selects for you or takes your keyboard: the overlays are pictures only, and
  joins and leaves are a quiet live-region note ("Heron left."), never a toast.
- A still pointer fades out after 5 s, and an away person's pointer isn't shown, so the canvas doesn't collect stale
  markers. A reload no longer leaves a second "you" for 30 s.
- Interim: connections a peer selects aren't outlined yet, and the alias edit (✎) in the chip's menu is still to come
  (backlog).
