## Paused admission lease review

This corrects capacity accounting and recovery when callers resume after live shrinks and outages. No controls,
text, menus, layout, focus or presentation chrome change. Request residence and execution remain
separate owners; returning unused execution capacity preserves the existing capacity contract.

Rejected retaining an unowned fractional slot, relaxing the admission oracle, changing R3's
ownership structure, or raising the shared-backoff test timeout. The minimal regression proves
the charged occupancy matches its owners during execution and after complete drainage. Additional
regressions require fractional capacity to remain usable and admitted continuations to finish ahead
of recovered continuations that need to reacquire request admission. This restores the existing
continuation priority without another quota charge or an admission bypass.
