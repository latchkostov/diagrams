The existing failure surfaces and Retry actions remain in place, with no new controls or chrome.
Room capacity and server capacity now have separate, accurate explanations. A numerical room
limit was rejected because the refusal does not advertise one; the successful welcome's capacity
cannot establish the limit for a rejected connection.

shortcut to change privacy, even when public share links are available for other diagrams.

exhaustion. Offline build regressions inspect emitted code and policy endpoints.
