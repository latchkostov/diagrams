## Resilience tutorial UX review

The lessons use the existing tutorial strip, Simulations picker, Live hover readings and Properties. No chrome
or app controls were added. Goals name the exact saved run or field. Watching traffic is sticky; switching runs
cannot reopen the unprotected-run step. Health checks waits through the outage rather than treating an initial
zero-loss reading as success, and also requires useful work at the surviving store.

Show me continues to reveal through the existing controller without selecting or moving focus. Skip, Continue and
selection perturbations run through the completion sweep, including the authored parts of the quarantined drafts.
The two owner-dependent lessons stay out of discovery and deep links. Replay and the final shedding interaction
remain pending rather than introducing controls or teaching an unapproved substitute.
