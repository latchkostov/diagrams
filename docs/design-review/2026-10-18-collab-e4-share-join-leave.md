## Edit together E4: Share's row, the chip and its menu, failure states

- One way in, where sharing already lives: Share's **Edit together…** sits after Link with password behind its own
  rule, says who can do what ("Anyone with the link can edit while you're there") and is never first. No new top-bar
  control: the chip replaces the save status only while a room is open, and its actions live in its menu, so it
  truncates its status rather than clip a control or wrap the brand.
- The chip's words carry the state without colour alone: "3 here", "Room open · waiting for others",
  "Reconnecting…", "Everyone else left · your copy is safe", "Could not connect". Its menu says who is here, names
  unverified, and offers only Copy room link and Leave room.
- Failures say why in plain words and always offer the way that works (a file), with Retry; they are announced and
  never take the keyboard. Waiting is never an error.
- Interim: the chip's menu is the top bar's menu (focus back to the chip), not the canvas's controller; "Left room ·
  your copy" in Your diagrams and the alias edit wait for E5 (backlog).
