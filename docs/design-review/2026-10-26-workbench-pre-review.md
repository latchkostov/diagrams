## Workbench pre-review UX review

The designer review checked primary-action prominence, the View menu budget and overlap behavior. View has 12 actions, within the existing limit, and Minimap announces an independent checkbox. It reuses the existing translated name and saved preference. The minimap is off for a fresh browser, without overwriting an explicit saved choice.

Play and Pause retain their dimensions and interaction states while using theme-aware primary ink. PNG samples show 16.58:1 in light and 15.44:1 in dark on desktop and phone. The final Edit Focus PNG puts the Checkout saga pin below the orders.events card. Pins and labels share occupied card geometry; no authored positions or camera rules change.

Rejected: retaining the green transport action (the owner's decision reserves that cue for Live), adding always-visible minimap chrome (View is sufficient), and painting a pin over a card when the frame is full (omit that pin until room exists). No budget or assertion tolerance was raised.

The older phone transport smoke test expected a visible rail in Live, contrary to step 11 and the completed phone behavior. Its replacement asserts the rail is hidden, transport spans the bottom edge, and AI still opens through its keyboard command. All other assertions remain.

The final PNG review found no material concerns in both Edit Focus themes and desktop/phone transport samples. All 18 shots retain the part-3 states, public examples and viewports; `report.json` in the capture folder records dimensions, hashes and direct PNG pixel measurements. The parity harness now explicitly selects its minimap fixture through View; the eight deliberate tidy-label changes are listed in the release fragment.
