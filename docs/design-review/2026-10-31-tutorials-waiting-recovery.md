## Scope and interaction

Reviewed the nine new/revised waiting and resilience lessons against the curriculum and
UX principles. No permanent editor controls are added. The coach uses existing Properties,
disclosures, exact fields, narration Continue, trace menu choices and expectations summary.
Reading acknowledgments remain explicit and do not claim to measure comprehension.

## Findings resolved

The required designer review found two blockers: held Ready/Testing readings still pointed
to Continue, and replay narration still claimed three partitions after the learner raised
the count. Reading and continuation now have separate instructions and targets; narration
uses wording valid for both runs. Explicit reading opportunities preserve controller
discovery before running. Prediction acknowledgment no longer claims to store an answer.
The late-work caveat appears before interpreting timeout success. Nested trace choices and
expectations summaries are reachable coach targets.

## Evidence and limits

The public KEDA policies and stories remain executable and unchanged. Practice narrows
the topology and freezes real simulation readings instead of adding timeouts or fake
counters. The poison rerun creates new work and checks an actual command trace at delivery
3; it never offers replaying dead letters. Finishes state the change, evidence and a
limitation or transfer question in two sentences. The designer review was static; browser
verification is recorded separately in the release/PR report.
