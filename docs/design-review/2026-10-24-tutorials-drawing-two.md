## Drawing tutorials, second batch

The six lessons use existing controls and add no editor chrome. Boundaries are taught through
selection, Ctrl+G, the inspector's Label, a drop and Fit to contents. The seed leaves enough space
between its first two services for the third. Annotation copy explicitly distinguishes zones
from boundaries and explains that neither text nor notes carry traffic.

The dependency lesson uses the canvas menu's Show → Dependencies because the teaching bar replaces
View. Arrangement follows the app's actual menu labels, Align → Align middles and Tidy up, rather
than inventing an Arrange submenu. Alignment accepts any selection of at least four services.

The Ctrl+K lesson checks its results, first adding a topic and then finding it again. Its add step
settles after selecting Service 1, so finding the topic is separate and Undo can still reopen adding.
Present keeps the real app chrome,
hides the teaching UI during presentation, and returns with Escape. Its hidden step state remains
available for deterministic solution playback. Share observes the actual dialog state and leads
with Interactive HTML, the first safe sharing row. Opening Share neither saves nor sends anything.

Tutorials never inspect DOM focus or move focus. Modified clicks, drags and box selection in the
generated tests wait for the canvas camera and remeasure their targets. Skip and Continue use
the same declarative demos, including sticky selection and alignment steps. Exit and explicit
Save as receive coverage using the new app-chrome lesson itself.
