## Readable instructions

The coach keeps prose in the normal text flow. Brackets may surround a sentence or its pseudo-locale translation; only individual marked key tokens form nonbreaking key chips. Nested explicit combinations remain recognizable without swallowing the sentence's opening bracket.

No control or chrome is added. Phone instructions, Done and Exit retain their original viewport, visibility and exact hit-target assertions, including the larger-font cases. A temporary hit-test wait was rejected once the trace established permanently oversized keycap markup; the product parser fixes the cause.
