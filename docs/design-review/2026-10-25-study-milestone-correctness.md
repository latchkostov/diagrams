## Design study completion review

Dinner rush rewards a fleet that handles the offered traffic rather than one whose growing backlog
briefly fits a loose threshold. Regression coverage plays the entire saved run for both the untouched
starting fleet and the reference fleet.

Skip reuses the learner's queue and waits for its connection through the runner's existing Restore
action before configuring the dead-letter path. Deferred connection commits are covered from both
editing and Live view. No new controls, chrome or runner forms are needed.
