## Conservative privacy and a fresh Restart

No controls or new copy. Privacy remains conservative while Undo can recover earlier content. Restart starts a fresh sandbox with no Undo or Redo; leaving or changing lessons disposes that history too. The canvas callback used by the on-device permission checkbox now updates temporary consent, which is discarded on Restart. Tutorials do not mount the AI panel; no new surface is added.

Rejected tracking provenance through individual history entries: sticky session privacy is simpler and avoids incomplete provenance restoration.
