## Load shedding UX review

The required designer-agent source review confirms that queue controls stay folded in the lazy
processor inspector and reuse Option H’s Field, PairedFields and Disclosure ledger primitives,
including the shared 10 px wide and 16 px narrow field rhythm. Connection priority uses the
existing dropdown. No global controls or particle shapes are added.

The review identified ambiguous configured-limit copy, total backlog being compared with a
pushed-work limit, and an unreachable processor hover explanation. These are corrected: the summary
says “Limit”, the queue has a separate pushed-work occupancy, and the existing Calls section explains
shed totals since restart. The hover also keeps a quiet historical fact. Current attention belongs
to the shared health window, not cumulative counters.

Additional toolbar controls, permanent health warnings from historical sheds, retry-first eviction
and a menu of unproven fixes were rejected. The callout retains one replay-confirmed action.
Browser layout and accessibility verification are pending at this WIP checkpoint.
