## Camera and title-band repair UX review

The owner decision remains intact: entering Live preserves a visible camera, and Follow reveals
the subject at the chosen zoom. No control, menu item or persistent setting is added.

The title band must exist before its deferred collision decoration downloads. A draggable name
cannot jump away between pointer movement and a press. Empty zones have no title ink to reserve;
their existing full-width strip remains available for dragging.

A phone reveal leaves space beside its subject for the narrated caller. Secondary narration cards
that do not fit become named pins rather than partially painted cards. The primary subject stays
painted even when the chosen zoom makes it wider than the pane. Hover details also clear painted
badges. Pins follow the remote endpoint's direction even when its route detours around other cards.
Animated zoom keeps title sizes readable in CSS and places collision bands when settled, without
delaying the camera; held manual zoom continues placing bands every frame. Existing overlap,
route-intersection, identity and camera-settling assertions remain strict.
