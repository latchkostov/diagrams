## Edit together E3: focus and view stay yours

- A peer's edit, rename or deletion never pans, zooms or refits your view, and never cancels a fit you started.
- Right-clicking a node that a peer then deletes: the menu closes (its actions would do nothing) and the keyboard
  is on the canvas, not lost to the page. The live region already says what was removed (R1).
- Joining: after Join the keyboard is on "Connecting to the room…" (its Leave is one Tab away), then on the canvas
  once the diagram arrives. Focus you moved elsewhere meanwhile (the top bar) stays where you put it.
- Room dialogs give the keyboard back to what opened them. Retry in the room chip keeps the keyboard on the chip's
  status as the button goes.
