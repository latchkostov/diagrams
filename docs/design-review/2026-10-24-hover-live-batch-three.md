## Hover and Live cards: batch 3 UX review

The required designer review found no blocking concerns. An idle worker no longer shows an invented thread warning; a worker whose occupied threads fill its pool keeps the warning. Queued demand during wake-up is not itself occupied threads. Other current health conditions retain their existing policy.

There are no new controls, copy, chrome, placement rules or focus/mode transitions. The existing shared segmented control preserves tuning on a no-op reselection. Browser coverage checks its persisted settings and Undo, while stronger readout assertions distinguish meaningful Live updates from stable but frozen UI.
