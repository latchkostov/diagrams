## Engine attempts R2 UX review

The existing Live controls and chrome are unchanged. A successful reply and downstream result now appear
at logical completion while sampled capacity can remain occupied. Down, drain and boundary outage no
longer contradict an answer already shown. Pausing defers the node's effects until resume.

The public blocking-call example exercises the corrected timing. No additional control, stencil, setting
or showcase is needed for this engine migration. The release-only tail preserves capacity readouts;
reservation replacement and CPU sharing remain the separately approved R3/R4 work.
