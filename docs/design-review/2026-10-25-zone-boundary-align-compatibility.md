## Integration UX review

No new controls or chrome. Existing node arrangement retains its call contract and geometry; boundaries still carry their selected descendants once. A switch from keyboard radius adjustment to dragging is a distinct Undo step, including in collaborative history.

Computed-style assertions cover the visible corners rather than only custom properties. The empty zone strip is measured in flow coordinates, dragged from its centre, and undone once; enclosed nodes stay put, preserving the distinction between zones and boundaries.
