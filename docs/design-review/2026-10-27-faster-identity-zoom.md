## Identity through camera gestures

The performance change adds no chrome, vocabulary or interaction mode. Fitted names and icons remain present: zooming in enlarges existing ink with its card, while zooming out conservatively steps its size. Four quiet frames restore exact sizing without depending on the browser's wheel-end timeout. Camera-owned fits keep their existing readiness contract.

The designer review caught issues addressed here: capture resume must invalidate cached identity, held input must permit fitting after a size step, and secondary text must wait for the effective identity scale when zooming from far to near. Unit coverage checks capture restoration and that visibility threshold; a browser regression samples Card, Compact and Icon only during held input across 15% to 75%, after fonts and initial frame detail are ready. The final UX review approved the change.

Layout/style containment preserves overflowing captions, focus decoration and shadows. Culling remains deferred because disappearing DOM would require a separate review of keyboard traversal, selection and Focus pins. Export parity remains unchanged.
