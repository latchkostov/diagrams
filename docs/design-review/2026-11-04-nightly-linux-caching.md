## Scope and review

No controls or new copy are added. Focus retains its existing readable neighbourhood and anchor; reserving decoration space prevents the first card from being hidden by the clipping policy. Overview, selection and manual camera ownership keep their existing commands.

Export keeps the input hold, progress, cancellation and natural drawing. SVG paint is applied to the detached copy, so exporting no longer temporarily restyles the live drawing. Native output ordering is preserved rather than accepting a new rendering or serialized format.

The cache runtime remains separate from Live's initial chunk. Non-Live entry points prepare their own dependencies, with fresh-realm coverage that cannot be satisfied by the ordinary unit setup's preinstalled runtime. Viewer coverage includes the restricted Confluence host policy.
