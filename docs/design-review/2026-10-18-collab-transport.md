## Collaboration E1 UX review

The Lab keeps its existing controls, layout, wording, disclosure and explicit Connect action. The endpoint defaults
show only the two approved services. An empty room is an ordinary waiting state and no longer raises the existing
failure banner or its recovery controls without a transport failure. Actual tracker/handshake failures retain the
existing Retry and file-copy path, with edits preserved. A pasted invitation cannot hydrate a competing document,
even when the creator checkbox is selected. No production surface or chrome-budget change is introduced.

Rejected expanding E1 into new status wording, lifecycle dialogs, endpoint controls, production CSP changes or TURN:
those are separate production-plan tasks. The transport exposes diagnostic states for those later surfaces.
