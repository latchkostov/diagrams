## Scope and chrome

Source review of observation ports and keyboard solutions against UX principles 2, 5, 6, 8 and 9.
There are no new learner controls, dialogs or layout changes. Existing product surfaces provide the
keyboard routes; the original pointer solutions and completion contracts remain in place.

## Review findings addressed

- Capture operation ownership before pickers, parser loads and writers wait. Prevent late imports,
  downloads, Checks results and joins after Restart, Exit or document replacement; preserve ordinary Open provenance.
- Consume momentary evidence per active step rather than certifying later matching steps with one action.
- Use F2 for Properties/name routes because Enter also serves narration and readouts.
- Open native select popups before navigating so intermediate simulations are not launched.
- Respect roving toolbars, menus and readout ownership; await actual focus changes rather than elapsed time.
- Keep literal pointer gestures out of the keyboard driver and retain the two explicit product dependencies.

## Validation and limits

Port publications, rejected states, writer completion, cancellation, source isolation and step-local evidence
have unit regressions, including deliberate regressions with the guards removed. Linux browser solutions
exercise actual controls and real completion checks. Two isolated collaboration peers use a deterministic
local tracker; AI uses a deterministic pasted reply. No account, external model or public matchmaking is
needed. These checks do not claim a screen-reader comprehension audit.
