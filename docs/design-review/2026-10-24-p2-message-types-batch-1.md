## Message types: batch 1 UX review

Reviewed the picker, Carries, focused-edge reader and bulk Emits against the UX principles. The existing inspector and focused edge remain the entry points; the top bar and floating chrome do not grow. Search appears only above the rendering limit and reaches omitted names without mounting the full vocabulary. Type chips retain native keyboard activation, per-value removal and a surviving focus destination.

Focused cards stay bounded and wrap long tokens. Escape dismisses the card before changing selection; a second press continues through canvas behavior. Two canvases keep their own text and focused cards. Appended rules remain visible after rekey Undo. Invalid bulk drafts preserve saved values until parsing succeeds, and the valid edit is one undo step.

Kept the existing lazy inspector and edge-reader surfaces, with their held-download and Retry coverage. Rejected an always-visible global search control and unbounded Show all expansion: both add cost to the common case without improving the local workflow. The new Lab preset uses two generic canvases solely to inspect reader ownership.
