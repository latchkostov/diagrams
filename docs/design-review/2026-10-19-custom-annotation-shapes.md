## Expanded annotation shapes review

Credit: @hfmm99 contributed the expanded shape annotation catalog and creation integration.

### UX principles alignment

- **Simple by default, customizable on demand:** The Inspector keeps the familiar 5 default shape buttons in the toolbar row, avoiding chrome clutter. The 6th "More shapes" button opens a categorized, searchable picker when deeper customization is needed.
- **Consistent discovery:** Extended shapes are searchable in the Palette, accessible from canvas context menus, and available from Quick-add (dropped connection and canvas double-click) without disrupting the quick stencil workflows.
- **Direct connection feedback:** Shape paths scale edge-to-edge within their node container bounds, guaranteeing that incoming and outgoing connector lines touch the visible outline with zero dead space.
- **Predictable geometry:** Corner resizing supports Shift-key aspect-ratio locking for perfect circles, squares, and equilateral shapes.
