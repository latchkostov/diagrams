## Live refresh and panel close

Reviewed against `docs/UX-PRINCIPLES.md`. The change adds no controls, settings or chrome.
Live's existing paths, dashes, theme colours, trace emphasis, cut links, visibility and pause
semantics remain the contract. Geometry follows route changes; style caches follow theme,
zoom, selection and decoration changes. A transitioning edge caches its destination opacity
instead of retaining its first faded sample. The exported viewer retains its existing
main-thread renderer and now omits the unreachable worker implementation.

Overview decoration skips hidden-label measurements and collision-home copies when a panel
closes. The camera retains its deliberate single bounds pass. Hidden badge updates no longer
remeasure settled cards.
Focus and Follow retain their placement rules. Browser coverage includes Live rendering,
hover cards, narration, transport, run history and both exported viewer formats; operation
counts guard the unchanged tick and hidden-label paths without a machine-dependent timing budget.
